Forest Collectibles Checker

Steam only tells you how many you have left. Load your save and this page lists exactly what you are still missing for the collection achievements: notes, blueprints, plated weapons, clothing, action camera videos, food, and crafted and printed items. It also shows where each one is on the map. The lists and positions come straight from the game's own files.

  1. Press Win + R, paste the path below and press Enter.
  2. Open your Steam ID folder, then SinglePlayer (or Multiplayer if you host, MultiplayerClient if you join someone).
  3. Open the save's number folder and pick SaveData.zip. Older saves have loose files instead: pick PlayerStateSaveData.json. You can also drop the file on this box.
%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Endnight\SonsOfTheForest\Saves

Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. The checker reads the save's player state: hasOwned for notes and clothing, DiscoverablePageUnlocked for blueprints, isPlated for weapons, consumed for food, crafted for crafted and printed items, and ActionCameraVideoCollected for videos. It checks one save at a time.

Item names are the game's internal names, made readable. A note or piece of clothing counts once you have picked it up. Blueprints count once they are added to your blueprint book. A weapon counts once it has been plated at the Solafite plater. Food counts once you have eaten it, and crafted or printed items once you have made one. Count-based achievements (logs placed, days survived, holes dug) are left out, since Steam already shows those numbers.
